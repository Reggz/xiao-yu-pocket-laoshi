# Brand Brief: Xiao Yu (小语)

## 1. Name and Tagline
- Brand Name: Xiao Yu (小语)
- Tagline: Your Pocket Lao Shi.

## 2. Mission
Kill the fear of speaking by providing a judgment-free, 24/7 Mandarin conversationalist.

## 3. Meaning of the Name
Xiao Yu (小语) means "Little Language". It signals intimate, bite-sized daily practice rather than heavy study.

## 4. Sub-Brand
"Pocket Lao Shi" is the job title: a professional mentor that lives in your pocket.

## 5. Personality
The "Cool Tutor" archetype: knowledgeable like a professor, chill like a friend.

## 6. Visual Identity
Minimalist "Neo-Chinese" aesthetic:
- Clean white space
- Bold typography
- Modern raindrop or speech-bubble mascot

## 7. Tone of Voice
- Encouraging
- Witty
- Slightly informal

Avoid AI-sounding phrases like "vibe" or "find your vibe." Use human phrasing like:
- "Quick start"
- "Starter check"
- "Let’s begin"

## 8. Target Audience
International nomads: professionals and students who find traditional apps too childish and textbooks too dry.

## 9. UX North Star
Frictionless entry. One tap to start talking. No public levels, no hearts, just flow.
Levels exist only internally for content gating.

## 10. Positioning
Not a Duolingo competitor. Xiao Yu is your best friend for language practice.
